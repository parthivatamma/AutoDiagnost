# Misfire isolation
For a cylinder-specific misfire, inspect the plug and coil, then swap the coil with a neighboring cylinder. If the misfire follows the coil, replace the coil. A flashing MIL indicates catalyst-damaging misfire; reduce load and seek service promptly.

# Lean conditions
Positive fuel trims suggest unmetered air, low fuel pressure, or inaccurate airflow measurement. Check intake hoses and smoke-test the intake before replacing sensors.
